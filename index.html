    Photo

    Take it from where you usually look at the garden, phone held level-ish. Your photo stays in your browser.

    Perspective

    Netgarden needs two things to put plants on the ground at the right size: where the horizon is, and one length you know.

    Drag until the line sits at the height your eyes were when you took the photo: for a fence about 1.8 m tall, a little below its top.

    Click two points on the photo along something you can measure: the back fence is ideal.

    Your garden

    Used for sun and shade checks. Facing south means the back fence is on the sunny side and shades the border in front of it.

    Things in front

    Paint over anything that should hide plants behind it: the existing shrub, the stone edge, a wall. Give each a distance.

    Realistic render

    Sends the current view to an image model that re-renders the planted areas so they look photographed in place. Needs your own OpenAI API key, which stays in this browser. Each render costs a few pence.

    Light

    Where the sun is decides where shadows fall. Look at an existing shadow in the photo and match it.
