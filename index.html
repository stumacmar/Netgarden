Three quick questions

1. Where was your eye level?

For a 1.8 m fence, a little below its top.

2. How tall is the back fence?

Tap the bottom of the fence, then the top of the same spot. A standard panel is 1.8 m.

Or mark a length along the ground instead
Check: drag the grey person around the lawn. If they look the right height next to the fence, the scale is right. The 1 m grid should lie flat on the lawn.
3. Which way is the camera facing?

Use the compass on your phone, standing where the photo was taken. This drives the sun and shade checks.
